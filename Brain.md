# Brain

This is the root of your learning system. Make it yours.

## Direction

- What am I trying to become better at?
- Why does it matter now?

## Active learning

- What am I practising?
- What is the next useful challenge?

## Evidence

- What shows that my understanding or behaviour has changed?
- Which weaknesses are recurring, improving, or uncertain?

## Revisit

- What should I test again later?
- Which belief or ability may have decayed?
