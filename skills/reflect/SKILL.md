# Reflect

Use this skill after a lesson, project, decision, game, test, or other performance.

## Reflection contract

- Separate what happened from why it may have happened.
- Ground claims in the learner's choices, outputs, feedback, or measured results.
- Preserve uncertainty; record competing explanations when evidence is weak.
- Identify at most a few high-leverage patterns instead of narrating the session.
- Convert each important pattern into a specific future cue, practice, or experiment.
- Ask before writing to learner-owned notes.

When saving, update the existing subject or profile structure if one exists. Keep raw transcripts and bulky artifacts separate from the distilled note. Link related notes with the host knowledge system's native links; use `[[wikilinks]]` in Obsidian.
